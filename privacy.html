Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

1. Scope

This policy covers this website and the personal software tools operated by Marek Mahdal ("I", "me") that connect to Google services, including the Google Cloud project used to connect a personal AI assistant to my own Gmail account (the "Tools").

The Tools are for my own use. They are not offered to the public, and I am their only user. No other person's Google account is connected to them.

2. This website

This website is a static page hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics or tracking scripts. GitHub, as the hosting provider, may log visitor IP addresses for security and operational purposes under its own privacy statement. If you email me, I keep your message for as long as our correspondence is relevant.

3. Google user data

The Tools access my own Google account through Google's APIs, with my consent given via Google sign-in. The data accessed may include the content, metadata and labels of messages in my Gmail mailbox, and the ability to draft, send, label or archive messages on my behalf.

This data is used only to read, organise and respond to my own email as I direct. It is processed on devices and services under my control. It is not sold, not used for advertising, not shared with third parties except the infrastructure providers needed to run the Tools, and not used to train general-purpose machine-learning models.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Retention and deletion

Email data is retained only as long as needed for the task at hand; cached copies are deleted when no longer required. Access can be withdrawn at any time from the Google Account permissions page, which stops all further access.

5. Security

OAuth credentials and tokens are stored on devices I control, protected by device-level security and encryption in transit. No method of storage is completely secure.

6. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time; the date at the top shows the latest revision.
